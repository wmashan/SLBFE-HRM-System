## 🔄 Complete Workflow

### Step 1: Employee Submits Online
- Employee fills HR/F/07 form online
- System shows instruction page
- Employee downloads HR/F/07 (auto-filled) and HR/F/08 (blank)

### Step 2: Physical Documents Submission
- Employee fills HR/F/08 at hospital
- Employee submits both forms + supporting docs to HR Division

### Step 3: You Mark Documents Received
1. Find claim in "Pending Documents" tab
2. Click **"Mark Documents Received"**
3. Confirm action
4. ✅ Employee automatically notified

### Step 4: Review & Decide

#### To Approve:
1. Click **"Approve"**
2. Enter approved amount (can adjust)
3. Add optional comments
4. Click **"Approve & Forward"**
5. ✅ Employee notified
6. ✅ Auto-forwarded to Finance Officer

#### To Reject:
1. Click **"Reject"**
2. Enter clear rejection reason
3. Click **"Reject Request"**
4. ✅ Employee notified with reason

## ⚡ Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: New Claim Arrives
```
1. Employee submits online form
2. Claim appears in "Pending Documents"
3. Wait for physical documents
4. When received → Mark Documents Received
```

### Scenario 2: Documents Arrive
```
1. Physical HR/F/07 and HR/F/08 received
2. Click "Mark Documents Received"
3. Employee gets notification
4. Claim moves to "Documents Received"
```

### Scenario 3: Ready to Review
```
1. Go to "Documents Received" tab
2. Click "View Details" to review
3. Check all information and documents
4. Decide: Approve or Reject
```

### Scenario 4: Approving a Claim
```
1. Click "Approve"
2. Enter approved amount (can be less than claimed)
3. Add comments (optional but recommended)
4. Click "Approve & Forward"
5. Done! Employee notified, sent to Finance
```

### Scenario 5: Rejecting a Claim
```
1. Click "Reject"
2. Write clear, specific reason
3. Click "Reject Request"
4. Done! Employee notified with reason
```

## ✅ Best Practices

### ✓ DO:
- **Review thoroughly** before approving/rejecting
- **Provide clear reasons** for rejections
- **Verify amounts** against policy limits
- **Check all documents** are present and valid
- **Add comments** on approvals for audit trail
- **Respond promptly** to pending requests

### ✗ DON'T:
- Approve without reviewing documents
- Reject without clear explanation
- Approve amounts over policy limits
- Delay processing unnecessarily
- Skip verification steps
